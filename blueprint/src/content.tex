\chapter*{Reading this blueprint}

This blueprint is deliberately detailed: intermediate lemmas and proof sketches
are retained as a learning aid. It follows the current Lean source rather than
silently identifying the formalization with every convention in the paper.
The separate \texttt{notes/formalization-ch1.pdf} gives a more extensive
mathematical companion to the proofs.

Numbered chapters follow the eleven sections of Guibas--Stolfi's paper.
Chapter 1 contains the subdivision theory, its surface examples, and the
orientability route toward the Figure 2 counterexample. The examples and
orientability work are supporting subsections of section 1, not additional
paper sections.

Chapters 2--11 are roadmap placeholders with the paper's section titles.
They provide locations to develop the rest of the blueprint without changing
this numbering. Existing algebra and permutation-splice code is noted in the
relevant chapters, but those chapters do not yet provide its full blueprint.

Nothing, yet.

\paragraph{Open work.}
Within chapter 1, Klein bottle nonorientability and nonequivalence of the two
subdivisions remain open. The proposed route needs excision, the plane local
homology computation, and comparisons between oriented atlases, local homology,
and the existing Klein bottle quotient. Future targets have no Lean declaration
attached and are marked not ready in the web dependency graph. In particular, a
proved negative determinant calculation is not treated as a proof of
nonorientability.

\paragraph{Status and assumptions.}
Completed declarations and proofs are marked formalized in the web version.
The graph records the displayed mathematical dependencies, not every low-level
Lean dependency.
The dependency graph currently describes the developed material and targets
in chapter 1. The later roadmap chapters have no proof nodes yet, so the graph
is not a completion chart for the entire paper.
